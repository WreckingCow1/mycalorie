My profile

Enter the details once. MyCalorie calculates an estimated daily energy target. It is an estimate, not a medical prescription.

If she is pregnant/breastfeeding, under 18, has a condition or medication that affects nutrition/weight, has a history of an eating disorder, or needs a medically prescribed diet, use a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on this calculator.

Food database

The built-in values are approximate everyday values. Packaged foods should be logged from the nutrition label when possible. The database includes South African staples and common foods.

Backup & restore

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. No account is required. Export a backup regularly if the data matters.

This app is designed to be free and local-first. It does not send food, weight or profile information to a server.
